Our Story

This company was born right under the Flat Irons in Boulder, Colorado with the dream of having sustainable and customizable hacky sacks. What makes our FootBags special is that they are filled and infused with herbs and essential oils that will smell good when you kick them or put it in your bag. Additionally, 50% of all our profits go to charities that help create a world we can enjoy.
